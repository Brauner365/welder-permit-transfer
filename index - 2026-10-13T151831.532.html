How the transfer works

A change of employer is a joint online application with the Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment (DETE). You fill in your part, the employer fills in theirs, and both of you sign. We guide both sides through it.

  1. Step 1

    Check and apply

    Use the checker below, then send us your CV, weld certs and permit details.

  2. Step 2

    Offer and contract

    Interview, offer, then a contract signed by you and the employer.

  3. Step 3

    Joint application

    We help you and the employer complete and submit it on the DETE portal.

  4. Step 4

    Permit reissued

    You start with the new employer once the updated permit is issued, and within one month of it.

Can you transfer?

Five quick questions, based on DETE's change of employer rules. Nothing you enter here is sent anywhere.

The rules in plain English

From DETE's guidance on changing employer under the Employment Permits Act 2024.

9 months first

You need at least 9 months since you started on your first employment permit in Ireland.

Same occupation

A General Employment Permit moves to the same type of job. A welder permit moves to a welding job.

2 months left

Your permit must be in force and have at least 2 months left when the application goes in.

No early start

You can't start with the new employer until the updated permit is issued. Starting early can get the application refused.

Up to 3 moves

A permit holder can be granted a maximum of three changes of employer.

No new labour market test

The new employer doesn't need to re-advertise the role first.
